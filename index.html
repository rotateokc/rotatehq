A few things to know

Glad you asked.

Who is Rotate for?

Rotate is for active fitness instructors who want to connect with peers, explore studios and recovery spaces, and make time for themselves. Instructor verification will be part of joining.

Are the classes free?

The plan is to offer complimentary classes for verified members at participating studios. Each studio will set its eligible classes and limits. Recovery offers and any related charges vary by partner. Rotate membership details and any fees will be shared before launch.

Does Rotate include recovery partners?

Yes. Recovery partners are part of Rotate alongside fitness studios. You'll be able to explore their offers as the partner community launches. Services, availability, and any charges will be set by each partner.

Where is Rotate launching?

We're starting with a small group of studio and recovery partners in the Oklahoma City area, then growing thoughtfully from there. Locations and launch timing will be announced as the pilot takes shape.

Can my studio or recovery business join?

Yes. We're welcoming fitness studios and recovery partners who want to build a more connected community. Select “I'm a partner” above to explore the opportunity and register your interest.
